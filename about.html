Designed for everyday questions

Vexel aims to keep the experience focused: enter a prompt, review the answer, and keep asking until the explanation fits your needs. A signed-in account can save your chat history so you can return to previous conversations.

Use AI thoughtfully

AI-generated responses can contain mistakes or omit important context. Double-check important claims and do not treat Vexel as a substitute for qualified professional advice. Avoid submitting passwords, financial details, or sensitive personal information in a prompt.

Frequently asked questions

What is Vexel AI?

Vexel is an AI chat web application designed to help with learning, writing, brainstorming, coding questions, and general exploration.

Can Vexel make mistakes?

Yes. AI responses may be inaccurate or incomplete. Verify important information with reliable sources.

Why might a response fail?

Temporary provider outages, network problems, or AI usage limits can interrupt a request. Try again after a short pause; repeated failures may require the site owner to check provider quota and service status.

Where can I learn how my information is handled?

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before creating an account.

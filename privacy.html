Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy describes how Vexel AI (“Vexel”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the website. The site owner should keep it accurate as the product, vendors, and settings change.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, Vexel collects the username and age entered in the sign-up form and uses an internal account identity for authentication. When you use the assistant, your prompts, supported attachments, and AI responses may be saved as chat history for your account.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide AI responses, show and save chat history, remember preferences, maintain the service, diagnose errors, and protect against misuse. Do not submit passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal details in prompts.

3. Service providers

Vexel uses Supabase for authentication and storage of signed-in chat history. Prompts and supported attachments are sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate responses. These providers process information according to their own terms and policies. The site may also load third-party advertising scripts when advertising is enabled; those scripts may use cookies or similar technologies according to the configuration and the provider’s policies.

4. Browser storage and installation

Vexel uses browser storage for session-related application state, preferences, drafts, and installation-banner dismissal. The website can also register a service worker to support the installable web app and cache selected application files. Clearing site data may remove locally stored preferences or drafts and may sign you out.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not describe chat content as public by default; signed-in chat records are associated with the account that created them. Information may be processed by the service providers above, where required by law, or as needed to protect the service. Stored information is retained while needed to provide the service and may remain in provider backups for a limited period according to provider settings. The site owner should publish an actual retention and deletion process before launch.

6. Your choices

You can avoid sending information in a prompt, sign out, and clear locally stored site data in your browser. For access or deletion requests about account and cloud chat data, contact the site owner through the project’s published support channel. The available process may depend on the service configuration and applicable law.

7. Children and age requirements

Vexel is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact the site owner so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted. Higher age requirements may apply in some locations.

8. Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used through the hosting and authentication services, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated, with the effective date changed when appropriate.

Important: This is a privacy-policy template, not legal advice. Before relying on it, the site owner should verify the exact data flows, advertising/cookie settings, retention periods, contact details, and applicable privacy laws.

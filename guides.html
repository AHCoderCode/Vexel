A simple workflow for almost any AI task

  1. Define the result. Describe what you are trying to finish, not just the topic you are asking about.
  2. Add the relevant context. Include the audience, level, input, environment, or constraints that could change the answer.
  3. Ask for a useful format. A checklist, worked example, comparison, short explanation, or code patch may be easier to use than a long paragraph.
  4. Review the result. Check important claims, test code, and ask the assistant to state assumptions instead of hiding uncertainty.
  5. Keep what you learn. Summarise the method in your own words and try applying it to a new case.

One important limitation

An AI response is not automatically true because it sounds fluent or includes a citation. For health, legal, financial, safety, or other high-impact decisions, use appropriate qualified sources and professionals rather than treating a chatbot as the final authority.
